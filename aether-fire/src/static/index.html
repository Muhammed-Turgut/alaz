What this shows

VIIRS (375 m) entered service on 2012-01-20. Counting its detections next to MODIS (1 km) produces an artificial jump in "burned area" unrelated to the fires themselves. The raw series breaks at the transition; the harmonized series (MODIS rescaled to VIIRS-equivalent hotspots before 2012, VIIRS counts afterwards) does not. The green line is the hidden counterfactual: what the SNPP record would have said before it existed.
